Fix Windows 11 Blue Screen of Death (BSOD) After Update

A Blue Screen of Death (BSOD) after a Windows Update is usually caused by a driver conflict, a corrupted update file, or an incompatible piece of software (like a third-party antivirus). Here is the step-by-step process to recover your PC.

Step 1: Boot into Safe Mode

If your PC crashes immediately on boot, you need to get into Safe Mode to fix it.

  1. Turn on your PC. As soon as the Windows logo appears, press and hold the Power button to force shut it down.
  2. Repeat this 3 times. On the third boot, Windows will enter the Automatic Repair environment.
  3. Click Advanced options > Troubleshoot > Advanced options > Startup Settings > Restart.
  4. When it restarts, press 4 or F4 to boot into Safe Mode.

Step 2: Uninstall the Problematic Update

If the BSOD started right after an update, removing that update is the fastest fix.

  1. Once in Safe Mode, open Settings > Windows Update > Update history.
  2. Scroll down and click Uninstall updates.
  3. Look for the most recent "Quality Update" or "Feature Update" based on the date.
  4. Click Uninstall and restart your PC normally.

Step 3: Run System File Checker (SFC) & DISM

Corrupted system files from a bad update process can cause the kernel to panic.

  1. Press Windows Key, type cmd, right-click Command Prompt, and select Run as administrator.
  2. Type sfc /scannow and press Enter. Let it reach 100%.
  3. Next, type DISM /Online /Cleanup-Image /RestoreHealth and press Enter.
  4. Restart your PC.

Step 4: Roll Back GPU Drivers

Display drivers are the #1 cause of BSODs (specifically error codes like VIDEO_TDR_FAILURE or SYSTEM_THREAD_EXCEPTION_NOT_HANDLED).

  1. Right-click the Start button and select Device Manager.
  2. Expand Display adapters.
  3. Right-click your graphics card (NVIDIA/AMD/Intel) and select Properties.
  4. Go to the Driver tab and click Roll Back Driver.
