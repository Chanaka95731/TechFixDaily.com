Android Apps Keep Crashing? Here's the Fix

You open an app, and within seconds, it kicks you back to the home screen. Or maybe you get a popup saying "App has stopped working". App crashes on Android are usually related to corrupted cache data or an outdated Android System WebView component.

Fix 1: Clear the App's Cache and Data

This is the most common fix. Over time, temporary files (cache) can get corrupted.

  1. Go to Settings > Apps > See all apps.
  2. Find the app that keeps crashing and tap on it.
  3. Tap Storage & cache.
  4. Tap Clear cache first, then try opening the app.
  5. If it still crashes, go back and tap Clear storage (Note: This will log you out and reset the app's settings).

Fix 2: Update Android System WebView

If multiple apps are crashing all at once (especially apps that load web pages internally like Gmail, Twitter, or banking apps), the Android System WebView component is likely the culprit.

  1. Open the Google Play Store.
  2. Search for Android System WebView.
  3. If it says "Update", tap it. If it says "Uninstall", tap that, then tap "Update" again to reinstall a fresh copy.
  4. Search for Google Chrome and update it as well (Chrome handles WebView on some Android versions).
  5. Restart your phone.

Fix 3: Check for System Updates

Sometimes the app has been updated for a newer version of Android, and your phone's OS is falling behind.

  1. Go to Settings > System > System update.
  2. Tap Check for update and install any pending updates.

Fix 4: Reinstall the App

If clearing the cache didn't work, the app installation itself might be corrupted.

Long-press the app icon on your home screen, select Uninstall, then go to the Play Store and install it fresh.
