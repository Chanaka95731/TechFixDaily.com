Mac Running Slow? Speed Up in 10 Minutes

Macs are known for their longevity, but eventually, the dreaded spinning beach ball of death appears. If your MacBook or iMac is taking ages to open apps or load web pages, follow these steps to clean it up.

Fix 1: Free Up Storage Space

macOS uses your hard drive as "virtual memory" when it runs out of RAM. If your drive is 95% full, your Mac will slow to an absolute crawl.

  1. Click the Apple menu () > System Settings > General > Storage.
  2. Wait for it to calculate. You need at least 15% free space for macOS to run smoothly.
  3. Click the 'i' icon next to Documents, Trash, or Messages to delete large, unused files.
  4. Empty your Trash! Files in the trash still take up space.

Fix 2: Manage Login Items

Too many apps launching at startup will make your Mac take forever to boot and waste RAM in the background.

  1. Go to System Settings > General > Login Items.
  2. Under "Open at Login", select apps you don't need immediately and click the Minus (-) button.
  3. Under "Allow in the Background", toggle off services you don't use constantly.

Fix 3: Reduce Motion and Transparency

Older Macs struggle to render the translucent windows and smooth animations of newer macOS versions.

  1. Go to System Settings > Accessibility > Display.
  2. Turn on Reduce motion.
  3. Turn on Reduce transparency.

Fix 4: Find Resource Hogs in Activity Monitor

  1. Press Cmd + Space, type Activity Monitor, and hit Enter.
  2. Click the Memory tab. Look for apps using GBs of memory. Chrome is usually the main offender.
  3. Click the CPU tab. Look for apps using high CPU %. Select them and click the X icon to force quit.

Fix 5: Update macOS

Apple regularly releases performance optimization patches. Go to System Settings > General > Software Update and install any pending updates.
