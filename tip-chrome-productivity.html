5 Chrome Hacks for Insane Productivity

Google Chrome is the world's most popular browser, but 90% of people only scratch the surface of what it can do. Start using these 5 built-in features to speed up your daily workflow.

1. Group Your Tabs Like a Pro

Stop drowning in 50 open tabs. You can group them together by project, color-code them, and collapse them.

Right-click on any tab and select Add tab to new group. Name the group (e.g., "Work", "Shopping", "Research") and pick a color. Now drag other tabs into that group. Click the group name to collapse/expand them instantly!

2. Search Specific Sites Directly from the Omnibox

You don't need to go to YouTube.com to search for a video, or Amazon.com to search for a product.

Type the name of the site into the address bar (e.g., youtube.com), and before pressing Enter, press Tab. You'll see the address bar change to "Search YouTube". Type your query and hit Enter to jump straight to the results.

3. Reopen Accidentally Closed Tabs

We've all done it: accidentally closing a tab you actually needed, losing the page you were just on.

Press Ctrl + Shift + T (or Cmd + Shift + T on Mac). Chrome will instantly reopen the last tab you closed, complete with its back-button history. Keep pressing it to reopen the last 10 tabs you closed.

4. Create Desktop Shortcuts for Web Apps

Turn web apps like WhatsApp Web, Notion, or Gmail into actual desktop apps that run in their own clean window without browser tabs or an address bar.

Open the website you want. Click the three dots menu in the top right > Save and share > Create shortcut.... Check the box that says Open as window and click Create. It will now act like a native app on your PC or Mac.

5. Search Your Open Tabs

When you have 40 tabs open across 3 different windows, finding that one Google Doc is a nightmare.

Press Ctrl + Shift + A (or click the little down arrow in the very top-right corner of Chrome). A search box will drop down allowing you to type the name of the tab you're looking for, instantly switching you to it.
