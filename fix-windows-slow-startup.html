Windows 11 Slow Startup Fix: Boot in 15 Seconds

Is your Windows 11 PC taking forever to boot? You press the power button, go make a cup of coffee, and it's still loading when you get back? You're not alone. Windows 11 has a lot of background processes that can severely impact boot times, especially if you're using an older SSD or HDD.

In this guide, we will walk you through the most effective ways to slash your boot time down to just 15 seconds or less.

Step 1: Disable Startup Applications

The number one reason for slow boot times is too many apps trying to start simultaneously with Windows. Let's disable the non-essential ones.

  1. Right-click on the Windows Start button and select Task Manager.
  2. In Task Manager, click on the Startup apps icon on the left sidebar (it looks like a speedometer).
  3. Look at the "Startup impact" column. Find apps labeled as "High".
  4. Right-click on apps you don't need immediately upon booting (like Spotify, Skype, EA App, Steam, etc.) and select Disable.

Note: Do not disable your antivirus or essential audio/network drivers.

Step 2: Enable Fast Startup

Windows has a built-in feature called Fast Startup which combines elements of a cold shutdown and hibernation to drastically reduce boot times.

  1. Press the Windows Key, type Control Panel, and hit Enter.
  2. Change the View by setting to "Large icons", then click on Power Options.
  3. On the left panel, click on Choose what the power buttons do.
  4. Click the link that says Change settings that are currently unavailable (requires admin rights).
  5. Under Shutdown settings, check the box for Turn on fast startup (recommended).
  6. Click Save changes.

Step 3: Adjust System Configuration (MSConfig)

We can optimize the boot process by reducing the boot timeout and enabling a "No GUI" boot which skips the Windows logo animation.

  1. Press Windows Key + R to open the Run dialog.
  2. Type msconfig and press Enter.
  3. Switch to the Boot tab.
  4. Check the box that says No GUI boot.
  5. Change the Timeout value from 30 seconds to 3 seconds.
  6. Click Apply, then OK. It will ask to restart, you can choose "Exit without restart" for now.

Step 4: Disable Unnecessary Services

Some third-party services significantly delay the desktop from loading.

  1. Open msconfig again (Win + R -> msconfig).
  2. Go to the Services tab.
  3. CRITICAL: Check the box at the bottom that says Hide all Microsoft services. (If you skip this, you might break Windows).
  4. Now look at the remaining list. Uncheck services belonging to apps you don't need running in the background all the time (e.g., Adobe Acrobat Update Service).
  5. Click Apply and OK.

Final Step: Restart and Test

Now, restart your PC. You should notice a massive difference in how quickly the lock screen appears, and how fast the desktop becomes usable after you type your password. If you applied all 4 steps, most modern PCs will boot in under 15 seconds.
